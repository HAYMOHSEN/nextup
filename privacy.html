Prioritize My Tasks privacy policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Prioritize My Tasks does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Your tasks stay on your device

The tasks you enter are saved in local storage on the device you use. They are never sent to the developer or to anyone else. Removing the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Backup files

A backup file is created only when you choose "Export a backup". It is saved where you choose on your own device and is never uploaded.

Network use

The app's own files are downloaded from its website when the app is installed or updated. Like any website, the hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as the IP address and time of a request. The app itself sends no information about you or your tasks.

Purchases

If you buy Prioritize My Tasks, the purchase is handled by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's own terms and privacy statement. The developer does not receive your payment details.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
